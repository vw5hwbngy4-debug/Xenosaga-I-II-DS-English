# Contributing / QA reports

The most useful contribution right now is runtime QA.

Public reference video:

https://youtu.be/WXCLkwqY2JU

For a bug report, include:

- emulator or real DS / flashcart setup;
- exact scene, menu or battle;
- what you selected/pressed;
- expected behavior;
- actual behavior;
- screenshot or short video if possible;
- whether the issue is reproducible after restarting.

High-priority reports:

1. freezes after dialogue choices;
2. text split inside words or outside textboxes;
3. Japanese mail/notification/menu text;
4. missing/garbled or difficult-to-read glyphs;
5. battle commands that cannot be selected or attacks that do not execute;
6. save/load problems;
7. late-game event crashes or untranslated UI.
